Software zero only
Each stream/channel subtracts its own first logged sample. No OnRobot BIAS/FILTER, no UR zero_ftsensor(), no URScript motion, and no TCP/payload writes.
Timestamp alignment
Best lag is -4.75 ms for URCap(t) - UDP(t + lag). Nearest UDP index deltas are mostly 4 packets (74057 transitions).
Conclusion
Strict first-zero equality is not supported. After timestamp alignment, the Fz residual keeps a fixed mean offset of -0.220 N (RMS 0.220 N, max abs 0.510 N), which is larger than force quantization and the remaining timestamp-alignment error. The dynamic shape is still consistent with approximately 4x downsampling/hold.
