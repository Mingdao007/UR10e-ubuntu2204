Next action

Stabilize contact entry before chasing higher frequency

The next Step2C change should reduce line-entry force transient: add a settle stage, or minimally lower normal velocity limit to +/-3 mm/s and alpha to 0.50. A formal OnRobot/Kunwei A/B needs same fixture, same zero/tare strategy, and same no-contact window.
